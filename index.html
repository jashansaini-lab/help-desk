Fiscal Year 2025 Internal Help Desk Operations Plan

Introduction

Hello! My name is Jaspreet Singh, and I am the Help Desk Supervisor for our internal IT Support Team. This plan outlines our operational goals and improvement strategies for the upcoming fiscal year to enhance our team’s performance, service quality, and user satisfaction.

Reason for the Plan

The purpose of this Operations Plan is to provide a clear roadmap for improving help desk efficiency, customer satisfaction, and service delivery standards. This plan is designed for the upcoming fiscal year to ensure measurable progress and alignment with organizational objectives.

Mission

Our mission is to deliver reliable, high-quality, and user-focused IT support services that empower all employees to work efficiently. We aim to resolve issues quickly, communicate clearly, and ensure every user interaction adds value to the organization.

Vision

To become a proactive, technology-driven support center recognized for excellence, innovation, and outstanding customer experience by the end of 2025.

SMART Goals for 2025

Goal 1: Customer Satisfaction

Increase Customer Satisfaction Level by 5% by December 31, 2025 through improved resolution quality, enhanced agent communication, and faster follow-up on unresolved tickets.

User Benefit: Users will enjoy faster, clearer, and more helpful responses, improving their overall support experience.

Goal 2: First Contact Resolution

Increase First Contact Resolution Rate by 4% by December 31, 2025 through better training, enhanced knowledge base, and Tier 1 agent empowerment.

User Benefit: Fewer callbacks or escalations, saving users time and ensuring quicker problem resolution.

Goal 3: Abandonment Rate

Reduce Abandon Before Answer Rate by 2% by December 31, 2025 by improving call routing, flexible staffing, and implementing call-back options.

User Benefit: Reduced wait times and quicker access to help desk agents during busy hours.

Goal 4: SLA Compliance

Increase Service Level Achievement for high-priority incidents by 6% by December 31, 2025 through optimized prioritization, improved escalation paths, and automated SLA alerts.

User Benefit: Ensures critical issues are handled on time, minimizing downtime and disruptions.

Goal 5: Speed to Answer

Reduce Average Speed to Answer by 10% by June 30, 2025 through better call forecasting, optimized staffing, and IVR routing.

User Benefit: Faster connections to agents and improved first impressions of IT support accessibility.
